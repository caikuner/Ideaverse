Thank you for providing the information. Here's a customized daily schedule for you to help improve your English skills, focusing on auditory learning and preparing for a job in an internet company. This schedule assumes you have 1 hour per day to dedicate to English learning.

## Daily Schedule

**Morning Routine (Before Work)**

- **7:00 AM - 7:30 AM**: Listen to an English podcast or audiobook related to technology or the internet industry. Some good options are "TechStuff," "This Week in Tech," or "Reply All."

**During Work Breaks**

- **Lunch Break**: Spend 15 minutes listening to short tech news updates in English (e.g., from NPR Tech News or BBC Tech). This will help you stay informed and improve your listening skills.

**Evening Routine (After Work)**

- **7:00 PM - 7:15 PM**: Listen to a TED Talk on technology or a topic related to the internet industry. You can find many talks on the TED website or YouTube.
- **7:15 PM - 7:30 PM**: Practice speaking by summarizing the TED Talk you listened to. You can do this by recording yourself or speaking to a language exchange partner.
- **7:30 PM - 7:45 PM**: Engage in an interactive listening activity, such as watching a tech-related video with subtitles and then without subtitles to test your comprehension. Websites like Khan Academy or Coursera offer free tech-related courses.
- **7:45 PM - 8:00 PM**: Review new vocabulary and phrases you encountered during the day. Use a spaced repetition app like Anki or Quizlet to help you remember these terms.

**Weekly Activities**

- **Join a Conversation Club or Language Exchange**: Once or twice a week, join an online conversation club or find a language exchange partner to practice speaking and listening in real-time.
- **Online Courses**: Enroll in an online course related to your field in English. Platforms like Coursera, edX, or Udacity offer courses on various tech topics.
- **Professional Development**: Follow English-speaking tech influencers on LinkedIn or Twitter, and engage with their posts to improve your professional vocabulary and network.

## Tips for Success

- **Consistency**: Try to stick to this schedule every day to build a habit.
- **Active Listening**: Focus on understanding the content and context when listening. Take notes if necessary.
- **Practice Speaking**: Don't be afraid to make mistakes. The more you practice, the better you will become.
- **Immerse Yourself**: Surround yourself with English as much as possible. Change the language settings on your devices to English, and try to think in English during the day.

Feel free to adjust this schedule based on your progress and feedback. Good luck with your English learning journey!
